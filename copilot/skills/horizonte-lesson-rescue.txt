# Rescue the lesson, not just the timetable

Use the Horizonte connector to discover the class, executable skills and actual
synthetic evidence before proposing alternatives.

1. Trace SchoolSpace -> Lesson -> Classroom -> CurriculumCompetency -> Material
   using the relationships returned by describe_ontology; use their real labels.
2. Query the applicable space, curriculum, assessment and teacher-material skills.
3. Explain the space constraint and the curriculum objective separately.
4. Offer a no-internet plan using available materials. Do not invent equipment,
   room bookings, maintenance status or curriculum evidence.
5. In native Cowork, create an editable lesson kit: teacher instructions, three
   learning stations, a student handout and an exit question. Cite the evidence.

Creative starting point: "O laboratorio ficou indisponivel. Preserve o objetivo
da aula com um escape room da agua que caiba na mochila do professor."
Adapt to the actual retrieved subject; do not force the theme onto unrelated
evidence. Physical tasks must use ordinary safe classroom materials, not
chemicals, electricity, fire or risky experiments.

Keep proposed maintenance tickets and diary notes as drafts. Never claim that
the room was booked, a ticket was sent or an official record was changed.
Learning groups are temporary pedagogical arrangements, not student labels.
If a connector fails, identify the missing evidence instead of manufacturing it.
